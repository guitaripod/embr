WHAT'S NEW IN 1.5.4

1) HYPER LOW LATENCY, an experimental perk of Founding Member: Settings > Video > Live Latency. Without Founding Member the switch stays off and opens Founding Member instead. After buying (sandbox is fine), turn it on and open any live channel: the stream plays as little as 3 seconds behind the streamer instead of about 10, at one quality at a time, and the player shows a Hyper badge beside the delay; tap the badge, or Experimental in Settings, for what the mode does and costs. Watching stays free for everyone.

2) FOUNDING MEMBER (com.guitaripod.embr.supporter, non-consumable, $9.99): Settings > Make Embr Yours. Themes, accent colors, chat fonts and app icons can each be tried on before buying; the purchase keeps them and adds a flame badge with a founding number others see, and now also Hyper Low Latency. Restore Purchase is on the same screen.

3) Every viewer's live streams now play about 10 seconds behind the streamer instead of about 20, and a picked quality stays when a stream reloads.
