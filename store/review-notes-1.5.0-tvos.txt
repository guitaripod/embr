EMBR STUDIO FOR APPLE TV (universal purchase with the iPhone and iPad app)

Embr on Apple TV is now Embr Studio, a screen streamers keep on while they're live: their own Twitch chat at couch size, the next ad break counting down, and every follow, sub, raid, hype train, poll and prediction as it happens. It plays no video. It reads only the streamer's own channel, or one they moderate, through Twitch's official Helix API and EventSub, with the permissions the streamer approves on Twitch's page.

NO LOGIN NEEDED TO SEE IT: on the welcome screen choose Try a Sample Stream. It shows an invented channel whose chat and moments play on a loop: follows, gifted subs, a raid, a hype train, a poll, a prediction and an ad break counting down, with Snooze and Run Ad. Every Studio Pro feature is unlocked in the sample. Swipe into chat and press Select on a message for its actions (pin, delete, time out, ban, report, block); the status line at the top has quick messages, chat modes and Shield Mode; Play/Pause freezes chat. Press Menu for Settings, where Leave the Sample Stream returns to the welcome screen.

SIGN IN (optional): Sign In with Twitch on the welcome screen, or Settings > Twitch Account, first lists the permissions Studio will ask for, then shows a code and a QR code. On a phone or computer, scan the QR or open twitch.tv/activate and log in by typing the demo username and password from the fields above into Twitch's own fields. Please do not use Twitch's "Continue with Apple", "Google" or "Amazon" buttons: they sign in with the reviewer's own account, not the demo account. Twitch then emails a one-time verification code, as it does on every sign-in from a new device; it appears within seconds at {CODE_PAGE_URL} (refresh the page). Enter it, check that the code matches the one on the Apple TV and choose Authorize; the Apple TV signs in by itself within a few seconds. The demo account's own channel (embrdemo) is offline, so Studio shows its offline screen with the next scheduled stream and the last recap; Twitch only gives ad-break controls to Affiliates and Partners.

STUDIO PRO (in-app purchases): auto-renewable subscriptions com.guitaripod.embr.studio.monthly ($3.99 a month) and com.guitaripod.embr.studio.yearly ($29.99 a year) in the group "Embr Studio Pro", and the non-consumable com.guitaripod.embr.studio.lifetime ($79.99). Settings > Studio Pro > Get Studio Pro, or select any lock inside Studio. Pro adds snoozing and running ad breaks, alert sounds, the stream recap, moderator mode for other channels and quick messages beyond the first; chat, alerts and moderating one's own channel stay free. There is no App Store free trial: a broadcaster's first live stream in Studio starts a free week of Pro inside the app, which ends by itself and charges nothing. The paywall shows each price and period, the auto-renewal terms and the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy addresses; Restore Purchases is on the same screen. One purchase covers Apple TV, iPhone and iPad.

FOUNDING SUPPORTER (in-app purchase com.guitaripod.embr.supporter, non-consumable): Settings > Founding Supporter. A one-time purchase that unlocks only looks: themes, accent colors, chat fonts, app icons on iPhone and iPad, and a flame badge in the buyer's color beside their name in chat, with their name in that color or glowing and a founding number. It unlocks no content and no features; Restore Purchase is on the same screen. Tips in Settings > Tip Jar unlock nothing.
