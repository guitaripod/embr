RIGHTS (Guideline 5.2.3): the Apple TV app plays no video and has no catalog or discovery of channels or content. It uses only Twitch's official developer platform (Helix API and EventSub), as a registered Twitch developer application, for the signed-in streamer's own channel. Evidence attached in App Review Information: Embr's registration in the Twitch Developer Console. Twitch Developer Services Agreement: https://legal.twitch.com/legal/developer-agreement/

Embr on Apple TV is now Embr Studio, a screen streamers keep on while they're live: their own Twitch chat at couch size, the next ad break counting down, and every follow, sub, raid, hype train, poll and prediction as it happens. It reads only the streamer's own channel, or one they moderate, with the permissions the streamer approves on Twitch's page.

NO LOGIN NEEDED TO SEE IT: on the welcome screen choose Try a Sample Stream. It shows an invented channel whose chat and moments play on a loop: follows, gifted subs, a raid, a hype train, a poll, a prediction and an ad break counting down, with Snooze and Run Ad. Every Studio Pro feature is unlocked in the sample. Press Select on a chat message for its actions (pin, delete, time out, ban, report, block); Menu opens Settings.

SIGN IN (optional): Sign In with Twitch on the welcome screen, or Settings > Twitch Account, first lists the permissions Studio will ask for, then shows a code and a QR code. On a phone or computer, scan the QR or open twitch.tv/activate and log in by typing the demo username and password from the fields above into Twitch's own fields. Please do not use Twitch's "Continue with Apple", "Google" or "Amazon" buttons: they sign in with the reviewer's own account, not the demo account. Twitch then emails a one-time verification code, as it does on every sign-in from a new device; it appears within seconds at {CODE_PAGE_URL} (refresh the page). Enter it, check that the code matches the one on the Apple TV and choose Authorize; the Apple TV signs in by itself within a few seconds. The demo account's own channel (embrdemo) is offline, so Studio shows its offline screen with the next scheduled stream and the last recap; Twitch only gives ad-break controls to Affiliates and Partners.

STUDIO PRO (in-app purchases): auto-renewable subscriptions com.guitaripod.embr.studio.monthly ($3.99 a month) and com.guitaripod.embr.studio.yearly ($29.99 a year) in the group "Embr Studio Pro", and the non-consumable com.guitaripod.embr.studio.lifetime ($79.99). Settings > Studio Pro > Get Studio Pro, or select any lock inside Studio. Pro adds ad controls, alert sounds, the stream recap, moderator mode and more quick messages; chat, alerts and moderating one's own channel stay free. No App Store trial; a broadcaster's first live stream starts a free in-app week that charges nothing. The paywall shows prices, periods, renewal terms and the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy; Restore Purchases is on it.

FOUNDING SUPPORTER (in-app purchase com.guitaripod.embr.supporter, non-consumable): Settings > Founding Supporter. A one-time purchase that unlocks only looks (themes, colors, chat fonts and a flame badge in chat), no content or features; Restore Purchase is on the same screen. Tips in Settings > Tip Jar unlock nothing.
