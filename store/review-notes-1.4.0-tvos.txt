EMBR FOR APPLE TV (universal purchase with the iPhone and iPad app)

NO LOGIN is needed to browse, watch and read live chat: open Browse, choose any stream and press Select. While watching: click once for the transport bar, which has Chat, Chat Options (side and text size), Quality, Favorite and Multiview; swipe down for the Info and Channel tabs; swipe up to switch to another live channel. Press and hold Select on any stream for Watch, Watch in Multiview, Add to Favorites and Open Channel. Past broadcasts replay their chat in sync.

SIGN IN (optional, adds the Following tab): Settings > Sign In with Twitch shows a code and a QR code. On a phone or computer, scan the QR or open twitch.tv/activate and log in by typing the demo username and password from the fields above into Twitch's own fields. Please do not use Twitch's "Continue with Apple", "Google" or "Amazon" buttons: they sign in with the reviewer's own account, not the demo account. Twitch then emails a one-time verification code, as it does on every sign-in from a new device; it appears within seconds at https://embr-demo-mail.guitaripod.workers.dev/code/bb7fef2e4b4103b5734ad317586553e7 (refresh the page). Enter it, check that the code matches the one on the Apple TV and choose Authorize; the Apple TV signs in by itself within a few seconds.

FOUNDING SUPPORTER (in-app purchase com.guitaripod.embr.supporter, non-consumable): Settings > Become a Founding Supporter. A one-time purchase that unlocks only looks: themes, accent colors, chat fonts, app icons on iPhone and iPad, and a flame badge in the buyer's color beside their name in chat, with their name in that color or glowing and a founding number. Every look can be tried on with the remote before buying. It unlocks no content and no features; Restore Purchase is on the same screen. Tips in Settings > Tip Jar unlock nothing.

TOP SHELF: move Embr into the top row of the Home Screen and open the app once; the Top Shelf then shows favorite and followed channels that are live, with previews (favorite a live channel first). With none live it shows Embr's Top Shelf image.
