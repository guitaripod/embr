WHAT'S NEW IN 1.5.0

1) EMBR STUDIO, for streamers: Settings > Embr Studio. Try a Sample Stream needs no login: an invented channel whose chat and moments loop (follows, gifted subs, a raid, a hype train, a poll, a prediction, an ad break counting down). Tap a message for its actions; the top bar has quick messages, chat modes and Shield Mode. Logged in, Open Studio shows the demo account's own channel (embrdemo), which is offline, so Studio shows its offline screen; Twitch gives ad controls only to Affiliates and Partners. Studio reads only the streamer's own channel, or one they moderate, through Twitch's official Helix API and EventSub with the permissions they approve on Twitch. It plays no video. The Apple TV app is the same Studio, submitted together as tvOS 1.5.0.

2) STUDIO PRO: auto-renewable subscriptions com.guitaripod.embr.studio.monthly ($3.99/month) and com.guitaripod.embr.studio.yearly ($29.99/year) in the group "Embr Studio Pro", and the non-consumable com.guitaripod.embr.studio.lifetime ($79.99). Settings > Embr Studio > Get Studio Pro, or tap any lock in Studio. Pro adds snoozing and running ads, alert sounds, the stream recap, moderator mode for other channels and more than one quick message. Chat, alerts, moderating your own channel and the sample stay free, and the sample unlocks every Pro feature so it can be seen without buying. No App Store trial: a broadcaster's first live stream in Studio starts a free in-app week of Pro that ends by itself and charges nothing. The paywall shows prices, periods, auto-renewal terms and Terms of Use and Privacy Policy links; Restore Purchases is on it. One purchase covers iPhone, iPad and Apple TV.

3) CLIPS FEED: a channel > Clips > tap a clip. Clips play as a vertical feed: swipe up for the next, down for the previous, tap to pause, Share and More (Report Clip, Hide) on the right, pull down to close.

4) CHATS TAB: several chats open without video, with unread and mention counts; up to three side by side on iPad. Mentions gathers messages that mention you from every open chat.

5) USER CARDS: tap a name in chat for avatar, account age and their messages, with Mention, Highlight, Block and Report.

6) MODERATOR TOOLS (opt-in): Settings > Safety & Moderation > Moderator Tools asks Twitch for moderator permissions; where the account moderates, a bar above the message box opens AutoMod's held messages, unban requests and the moderation log, and user cards add Delete, Time Out and Ban (try the embrdemo chat).

7) FOUNDING SUPPORTER (com.guitaripod.embr.supporter, non-consumable): Settings > Become a Founding Supporter. Unlocks only looks (themes, accent colors, chat fonts, app icons, a flame badge with a founding number others see), each tried on before buying. Restore Purchase is on the same screen.

The app supports iPhone and iPad: on iPad, video and chat sit side by side in wide windows and stack in narrow ones.
