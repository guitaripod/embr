SIGNING IN WITH THE DEMO ACCOUNT (Twitch)
Embr has no Sign in with Apple. Twitch's login page, which Embr opens, shows Twitch's own "Continue with Apple", "Google" and "Amazon" buttons: please do not use them. They sign in with the reviewer's own account, which is not the demo account, so Twitch shows an error or offers to create a new Twitch account. Type the demo username and password into Twitch's fields instead.

Twitch then emails a one-time verification code, as it does on every sign-in from a new device. Twitch has no setting to turn this off and no fixed code. The demo account's email arrives on this page, which shows the newest code within seconds and refreshes itself:

1. In Embr: Connect Twitch Account on the welcome screen, or later Settings > Log In with Twitch (the card at the top of Settings).
2. On Twitch's page, type the username and password from the fields above and tap Log In.
3. When Twitch asks for the verification code, open the link above in Safari on any device. The code appears within seconds of Twitch asking for it, with how long ago it arrived.
4. Enter that code on Twitch's screen to finish signing in.

NO LOGIN is needed for browsing, search, watching, reading live chat and VOD chat replay. Login adds Following, sending chat and moderation.

WHAT'S NEW IN 1.5.0

1) CHATS TAB: the Chats tab keeps several channels' chats open without their video, with unread and mention counts on each; Open in Chats is on every channel's menu. On iPad, up to three chats sit side by side. Mentions, at the start of the Chats strip, gathers messages that mention you or match your highlights from every open chat.
2) USER CARDS: tap a name in chat for that chatter's card: avatar, account age and their messages in this chat, with Mention, Highlight, Profile, Copy, Block and Report.
3) MODERATOR TOOLS (opt-in): Settings > Safety & Moderation > Moderator Tools. Turning it on asks Twitch for the moderator permissions. In channels where the signed-in account is a moderator or the broadcaster, a bar above the message box opens a queue to allow or deny AutoMod's held messages, answer unban requests and read the moderation log; the user card adds Delete, Time Out, Ban and Unban there. The demo account is the broadcaster of its own channel (search for embrdemo), so in that chat a user card shows Delete, Time Out and Ban.
4) FOUNDING SUPPORTER (in-app purchase com.guitaripod.embr.supporter, non-consumable): Settings > Become a Founding Supporter. A one-time purchase that unlocks only looks: the OLED Black, Midnight, Ember and Dusk themes, seven accent colors, four chat fonts, eleven app icons, and a flame badge in the buyer's chosen color beside their name in chat, with their name in that color or glowing and a founding number, which other Embr viewers see. Every look can be tried on before buying. It unlocks no content and no features; watching, chat and everything else stay free. Restore Purchase is on the same screen.
5) ALSO: Settings is redesigned as pages like the Settings app, with search; favorites sync between the person's iPhone and iPad through their own iCloud; a new message box with swipe to reply.

The app supports iPhone and iPad: on iPad, video and chat sit side by side in wide windows and stack in narrow ones.
